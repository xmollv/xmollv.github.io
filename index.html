About

I'm a Senior iOS Developer who has been building iOS apps for the past 10+ years. I've worked in a wide variety of companies, from very small teams — even shipping apps completely by myself — to large teams with massive codebases. I've built social media apps, chat apps, enterprise apps, photo/video capture apps, public SDKs, and more. I'm an expert in Swift, UIKit, Core Data, SwiftUI, and Swift Concurrency. I thrive in startup environments where people take ownership, move fast, and help each other out. I’m always looking for ways to improve the foundations, raise the quality bar, and take the product to the next level.
