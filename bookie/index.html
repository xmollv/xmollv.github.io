Bookie for iPhone

Every book you love.
Every quote you keep.

Bookie is a calm, private reading journal. The books you want to read, the one on your nightstand, and every book you've finished, together in one beautiful library.

Download on the App Store ↗

Free for up to five books. No ads, no tracking, nothing to sign up for.

A calm home.
For everything you read.

📚

Three shelves for your reading life

Keep your books in Pending, Reading, and Read. Search by title, author, or both, preview any book, and add it to a shelf. Can't find one? Add it yourself, with your own photo as the cover.

📖

A page for every book

Every book gets its own page, tinted with the colors of its cover. Bookie notes the day you start and finish each book, and tells you how long it took.

✍️

Make each book yours

Remember who recommended it and write down what you thought. Browse covers to find the edition you own, and edit the dates, page count, and more.

🔖

Keep the quotes that move you

Scan a passage right off the page with your camera, or type it in. Keep the page number and your own thoughts beside every quote, and find any of them by its words, its book, or its author.

🖼️

Quotes worth sharing

Turn any quote into an image in three styles: Cover, Night, and Paper. Pick Story, Square, or Landscape, then share it anywhere or save it to Photos. Cards show the quote and its book, never your notes.

📊

Watch your reading grow

Books and pages finished, all time or year by year. See how long books take you, how long they wait on your pile, and your most-read authors, genres, and languages.

🔒

Private by design

Your books, quotes, and notes live on your iPhone and sync with your iCloud. Your library works offline, and you can export all your books and quotes as text, anytime.

💜

Bookie Pro

Bookie is free for up to five books, with quotes, stats, and quote cards included. Pro adds an unlimited library and quote cards without the Bookie footer. What you've saved always stays yours, with or without Pro.
